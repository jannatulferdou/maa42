Maa42

AI Maternal Companion for Safer Motherhood

Maa42 is an AI-powered maternal health companion designed to support women during pregnancy and the first 42 days after childbirth through early risk detection, recovery monitoring, and timely care guidance.

AI Risk Assessment

Identifies possible maternal danger signs from symptoms and health inputs.

Offline First

Designed for low-resource areas where internet access may be limited.

Recovery Monitoring

Supports postpartum follow-up during the critical first 42 days.

Emergency Guidance

Provides timely guidance when urgent medical attention may be needed.
